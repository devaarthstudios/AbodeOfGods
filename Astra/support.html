Support

Everything about how Abode Of Gods : Astra works.

How to play

  1. Tap FIGHT on the menu to start the current level. TRAINING lets you practise.
  2. Move with the arrow buttons (or the joystick, see Settings). ATK attacks, BLK blocks, DSH dashes and KIK kicks.
  3. Empty your opponent's health bar before yours runs out.
  4. Win to unlock the next level. There are six levels, each against a different opponent with a different weapon.

Settings

  • Controls: switch between buttons and a joystick. Your choice is remembered.
  • Sound: turn sound on or off. Your choice is remembered.
  • Credits and licences: credits for the game and open-source licence notices.

During a fight

  • The pause button at the top right freezes the game.
  • Fights are one-on-one and short. Your level progress is saved on your phone.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need the internet?
No. The game works fully offline and has no internet permission.
Is there any purchase or advertising?
No. There are no ads and no in-app purchases.
Where is my progress saved?
Only on your phone, in the app's private storage. Uninstalling the game deletes it.
