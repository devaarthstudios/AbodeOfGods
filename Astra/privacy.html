Privacy Policy

Abode Of Gods : Astra by Devaarth Studios

Last updated: October 24, 2026

1. Information collected by the game

Abode Of Gods : Astra does not collect or share personal information. There are no accounts or sign-in features.

2. Information stored on your device

The game saves your sound setting, control style (buttons or joystick), unlocked levels and current level in the app's local storage. The game does not send this information to us. You can clear it through Android's app settings.

3. Internet access and permissions

The game works offline. This version requests no Android permissions, including no Internet permission. It does not access your camera, microphone, location or contacts.

4. Ads and analytics

The game contains no ads, analytics tools or third-party tracking services. There are no in-app purchases.

5. Children's privacy

The intended audience is ages 13 and over. The game does not collect personal information from players.

6. Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address and any information you choose to send, and use it to respond to your request. Please do not send passwords or other sensitive information.
